How this must be built

Required for a real private gallery

Requirement 01

Authentication

Server-side sessions or JWTs, bcrypt/argon2 password hashing, rate limiting and lockout on repeated failures. Magic-link email sign-in is a good alternative to passwords.

Requirement 02

Authorisation

Every request must check that the signed-in user actually owns the gallery being requested. Client A must never be able to guess or open Client B's URL.

Requirement 03

Signed, expiring URLs

Serve images from object storage (S3, Cloudflare R2) via short-lived signed URLs. Never expose a predictable public path to private files.

Requirement 04

Encrypted storage

Encrypt the archive at rest and in transit. Keep at least two geographically separate backups of every delivered collection.

Requirement 05

Watermarking & rights

Optional preview watermarks, download limits, and an auditable record of what each client downloaded and when.

Requirement 06

Data protection

Comply with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023: a privacy notice, consent records, data-retention limits and a documented deletion process on request.
