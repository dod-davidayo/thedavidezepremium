Delivery & Turnaround

From shutter
to your hands.

Stage 01

Backup & ingest

Every shoot is copied to two independent drives on the same day, plus the in-camera cards. Nothing is formatted until three copies exist.

Stage 02

Cull & select

Images are reviewed at full resolution and the strongest frames selected. Technical duplicates, blinks and missed focus are removed without sentiment.

Stage 03

Colour & grade

Each selected image is graded by hand to the studio's signature look — warm neutral tones, controlled contrast, faithful skin.

Stage 04

Retouch

Selected frames receive careful retouching: distractions removed, exposure balanced, never to the point of looking like a different person.

Stage 05

Gallery build

A private online gallery with individual downloads, favourites, sharing links and print ordering is prepared and sent to you.

Stage 06

Albums & prints

Where included, albums are designed for approval, then printed on archival paper and hand-bound. Framed prints ship separately.
