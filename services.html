Transparent Terms

How we work,
in writing.

Terms 01

Quotations are bespoke

Every commission is quoted individually. Final pricing depends on the event, location, duration, guest numbers and specific requirements — the figures here are a starting point only.

Terms 02

Deposit secures the date

A booking deposit — 40% in the current configuration — reserves your date exclusively. The balance is due before delivery of final files.

Terms 03

Delivery is scheduled

Each package states its delivery window. Rush delivery is available as an add-on where the studio schedule allows.

Terms 04

Editing is included

Colour grading, contrast and finishing are included on every delivered image. Advanced retouching beyond your package allowance is billed per image.

Terms 05

Licensing is explicit

Personal-use print and sharing rights are included. Commercial, advertising and exclusive usage must be licensed in writing before publication.

Terms 06

Cancellation is fair

Rescheduling is supported with reasonable notice. Read the full cancellation and rescheduling policy on the Client Care page.

!

Important. Quotations depend on event, location and requirements. Nothing on this page constitutes a binding offer. Availability is confirmed only by the studio, in writing, after an enquiry is received.
